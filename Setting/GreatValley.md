# The Great Valley
> *In the greater region of Myrath, nestled in between the mountains and hills before the Weathered Walls, lie the Great Valley. Home to the People of Earth and Flame. Menders of the Burning Wounds left behind by the Great Searing.*
> 

The Great Valley is surrounded by mountains to all sides except a pass in the south leading towards the coast and the seas beyond. The valley is warm, misty and fertile, showered by the rising clouds crashing with the weathered walls. The rain flows down in rivers, settling in several great lakes. It is here the people make their home, among great beasts, and ancient legends they feed of the land and work to appease the elder things that move and warp the world to their unknowable designs.
