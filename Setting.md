# The World of Velherak
Velherak is a vast mythical Stone Age world wrapped tight in the embrace of terrible and strange gods, monsters and otherworldly entities.

The world itself is vast and beyond comprehension as paths vanish and re-appear, folk wander across small islands for decades not finding shore and hidden beyond sight, small rivers are in fact great floods.
